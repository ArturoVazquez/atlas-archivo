    Locations

    The institute has four main locations in France and Spain. Our headquarters are located in Grenoble, France.

     

    The Grenoble site hosts the main development and technical services as well as being the base station for the NOEMA observatory.

     

    The Granada site hosts our Spanish administration and the support for the 30-meter telescope on Pico Veleta.
